# Art and release finishing brief

The native builds establish editable products and working controls. The previews do not yet establish the close-up realism, optimization or complete environment presentation needed for a premium cinematic release. Use this brief alongside the Unreal handoff when continuing in a DCC or engine.

## Observations from this edition

- The nine standalone previews show the actual delivered geometry on a simple included ground. Their consistent lighting is useful for comparison; add front, back, detail and wireframe views before sale.
- Tree silhouettes vary, but the broadleaf branch transitions, bark, needle/leaf anatomy and palm trunk detail need close-up art review. High polygon counts alone do not establish realism. There are no validated LODs or physical wind systems.
- The isolated mansion overview exposes disconnected site components and offshore context without its surrounding estate terrain/water. Resolve the standalone scene boundary and ground treatment before publishing a mansion hero render. The local diagnostic render is not used in the catalogue.
- The estate opens with 53 cameras and packed generated textures. Opening the file and checking dependencies does not verify every room, camera or material.
- The current tree, gate and lighting animations are useful Blender controls. Real-world simulation, structural accuracy, collision and engine interaction have not been validated.

## Copyable production prompt

> Continue the private Altair product edition without editing Car Mansion or adding third-party models, scans, brand assets or reference photographs to deliverables. Work from the supplied native files and preserve the original generator backup. Treat the catalogue's proposed prices as planning values until the work below passes review.
>
> Finish one standalone product first. Inspect it from all sides at its intended camera distance. Correct visible intersections, scale errors, abrupt branch joints, repeating patterns, floating components and unsupported details. Preserve the existing useful controls and test after every geometry change. Show a neutral-light comparison before and after each material improvement; do not substitute an enhanced image for delivered detail.
>
> For vegetation, use independently created botanical geometry and materials. Validate leaf and needle shapes for the named species, branching hierarchy, trunk taper, leaf distribution and underside appearance. Give each variant a useful trunk-base pivot. Produce hero and lower-detail versions only when their appearance and performance have been checked. Document triangle counts per variant and measured memory use.
>
> For the standalone mansion, define exactly which rooms, terraces, foundations and gardens belong in the product. Separate optional offshore/context objects into a clearly named presentation collection; remove accidental floating context from the architectural overview. Add an owned ground/shoreline treatment or a deliberate clean cutaway presentation. Check every stair, door, glass surface, furnishing placement and lighting transition using eye-level cameras. For the estate, verify the same boundaries in context, including water and terrain seams.
>
> Produce native 4K stills, detail views, a short walkthrough and a control demonstration directly from the delivery files. Avoid presenting camera positions as an animated sequence until actual shot timing and transitions exist. Report render settings and hardware. Make a copy in a clean project, open it with automatic script execution disabled, test the controls, and check for missing assets. Record the results against the exact file checksum.
>
> Deliver the revised native packages, previews, updated manifests and a list of remaining limitations. Keep editable files and generators in private storage. Publish only truthful catalogue media and metadata after review; do not activate checkout, deploy contracts or mint collectibles as part of this finishing task.

## Commercial sequence

Start with a small architectural product whose buyer workflow is easy to demonstrate. Use actual evaluator feedback to settle the price and documentation. Release additional packs after the same checks; treat the mansion, estate and any native Unreal edition as separate milestones. Marketplace acceptance and demand remain unverified until the seller checks current rules and tests the offer with its intended audience.
