VI

Why this stone

Each surface, and why it is that one

Every surface a camera comes close to is real geometry or a scanned material. Why each one:

  • River pebbles in lime mortar

    69,793 of them, each set by itself, the brick showing through

    C16

  • Split-face limestone

    Real relief, so a low sun catches every face

    X4

  • Basalt

    Near-black blocks, each face leaning its own way, sharp joints

    H13

  • Chalk

    Scalloped hollows between sharp ridges

    H10

  • Schist

    Fissured slabs, the plates dipping steeply

    H16

  • Sandstone

    Eroded ledges that follow its colour beds

    C18

  • The pool's water

    It absorbs instead of mirroring, so the sun reaches the car through it

    C17

  • The dining room's marble

    White squares on the diagonal, black cabochons, a black band

    H17

  • Turner's shipwreck

    In the public domain, hung at its own proportions, its brushwork in relief

    H08

  • Lavender

    In rows down the slope, a path between them

    C23
